BEFORE YOU START

A few useful answers.

What happens when I add my website?

We crawl up to 20 public pages, capture a network baseline, and run six analyses concurrently — company intelligence, SEO, AI visibility, competitors, audience, and content. The first document lands in under three minutes.

Does Smark publish anything automatically?

No. Agents surface opportunities and prepare work. You keep approval control over everything.

Which AI providers are supported?

OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, and OpenRouter. You connect your own key during setup and can change providers any time.

What export formats are available?

Every analysis produces a branded PDF, an editable PowerPoint, and — when the data warrants it — an operational Excel workbook with trackers and scores.

How far does the token budget go?

The default workspace budget is 2M tokens. A full company scan uses roughly 180k–300k tokens depending on site size. Plan for 6–8 complete scans, with focused edits using substantially less.
